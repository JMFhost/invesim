Investing Guides

InveSim is a game — but the investing it teaches is real. These four guides distill the investing books of Jesse Pham into practical lessons you can practice immediately in the game, with virtual money and zero risk. Learn how to invest for real: how to think about markets, how to evaluate businesses, and how to generate income from the assets you own, and how to escape the salary trap and become an owner. Then open the game and prove each lesson to yourself.

Start with An Actual Investor for the mindset, Think Like an Owner for the evaluation method, and Premium Harvest for income strategies. Or jump straight to the one that matches what you want to practice today. Escape the Gilded Cage is the journey itself: breaking out of the salary trap and becoming an owner.

An Actual Investor

By Jesse Pham

What you will learn: why most traders lose money, how the trading industry profits from your activity, the brutal mathematics of losses and compounding, how to evaluate a stock like a business, what economic moats are and why they matter, and why patient money beats stupid money every time.

Practice in InveSim: the Stocks app, the Portfolio app, and the discipline of holding quality businesses through market noise.

Think Like an Owner

By Jesse Pham

What you will learn: the complete method for evaluating a stock as a business — business economics and return on capital, reading the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement, price versus value and the margin of safety, P/E and discounted cash flow valuation, management and competitive position, financial health, portfolio construction, and the common mistakes that destroy returns.

Practice in InveSim: evaluating any of the game's 15,025 stocks using the fundamentals on every stock page — P/E, margins, ROE, debt, growth, intrinsic value, and quarterly reports.

Premium Harvest

By Jesse Pham

What you will learn: how to stop gambling on options and start collecting premium like the house — covered calls, protective puts, and cash-secured puts, position sizing and risk management, advanced spread strategies, the psychology of not sabotaging yourself, adapting to bull, bear, and sideways markets, and building a systematic options income business.

Practice in InveSim: the Options system inside the Stocks app — buy and write calls and puts with real Black-Scholes pricing, Greeks, and expirations from 7 days to 720-day LEAPS.

Escape the Gilded Cage

By Jesse Pham

What you will learn: why a stable salary is a gilded cage — and how to escape it: the salary, debt, and fear formula, the Freedom Number, reading a business like a story, moats and valuation, when to buy and sell, portfolio building, index funds, starting early and staying consistent, harvesting income, mastering your own psychology, lessons from Berkshire Hathaway, retailers, and tech versus telecom, real estate without being a landlord, the sweet traps of MLPs and covered call ETFs, and teaching your children the language of money. Translated and adapted from the Vietnamese original Thoát Khỏi Lồng Son.

Practice in InveSim: the Job app, the Stocks app, the Portfolio app, the Bank app, the Real Estate and eREITs apps, and the discipline of building income that keeps flowing when you stop working.

Learn for real, risk nothing: InveSim uses virtual money only. These guides are education, not financial advice, and nothing in the game is a recommendation to buy or sell any real security.
