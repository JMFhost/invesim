Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers about how InveSim works, drawn from how the game actually behaves.

Is InveSim real money?

No. InveSim uses virtual money only. You start with $10,000 of simulated cash on Day 1, and every stock, property, business, and option in the game is simulated data — not real securities, not real prices, and not connected to any real market. Nothing in the game is investment advice, and nothing you do here involves real money, real accounts, or real trades.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup, no login, and no account. Open the game and play. Your progress is saved automatically in your own browser (see the saves question below).

How do saves work?

The game autosaves continuously in your browser using local storage — a fast autosave runs every game minute and a full snapshot is saved periodically and when you leave the page. Your save lives only in this browser on this device: there is no cloud save and no cross-device sync. If you clear your browser's site data, your game is gone, so use the manual tools in Settings → Save & Load: three manual save slots (Save / Load / Delete), plus Export (downloads a JSON file you can keep) and Import (restores a game from a file you saved). The export file is named with your seed and day, like investsim_seed_123456_day_45.json. To move a game to another device, export the file and import it there.

Can I play on more than one device?

Only by moving a save file yourself. There is no account and no sync, so a game started on your phone will not appear on your computer. Use Settings → Save & Load → Export on the first device, then Import on the second.

How does game time work?

One real second equals one game minute. Each game day runs from 8:30 AM to midnight. The clock keeps running in the background while the tab is hidden, and catches up when you return (catch-up is capped). One game year is 252 trading days. The +1 Day button on the home screen fast-forwards directly to 8:30 AM the next day. The status bar shows the current game day and time; tapping it opens the Notification Center. You can also enable Autoplay in Settings to let days advance on their own.

When can I trade?

Stocks and ETFs trade from 8:30 AM to 7:00 PM game time. Options trade from 8:30 AM to 3:00 PM. Real estate trades from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Outside those hours, trading is blocked with a message. Forex, commodities, eREITs, small businesses, venture capital, and the casino have no market-hours restriction — they are available around the clock.

How do taxes work?

The game charges a flat 30% tax once per game year (every 252 trading days) on your job salary plus net investment profit — gains minus losses, including depreciation shields from property. The tax bill is due with a grace period of 84 trading days (about four months). If you don't pay in time, a 20% penalty is added and the tax authority seizes assets to cover the bill: cash first, then stocks, ETFs, managed accounts, and more. If seizure still can't cover what you owe, you go to jail — and jail earns a $50-per-day credit against the bill until it's paid off, at which point you're released. You can pay taxes early from the Bank app, and there's a tax-loss-harvest button that sells all your losing long stock positions to book losses against gains. A good habit: keep a 5–10% cash reserve before year-end so the bill never catches you short.

What is margin, and what happens in a margin call?

Margin is optional borrowed money for investing, and it starts OFF. When enabled, you get 2x Reg T margin: your buying power becomes your cash plus a margin credit, and borrowing costs the Fed rate plus 2.50% per year, accrued daily. If your equity falls below 25% of your total assets, a margin call triggers: the broker automatically sells 50% of each stock position to pay down the debt. Margin amplifies both gains and losses — the same leverage that doubles a win doubles a loss and can force sales at the worst moment.

Can I go to jail? What does it do?

Yes — jail happens when a tax bill can't be covered even after asset seizure. While in jail, investing is broadly blocked, casino work and lottery tickets are locked, and your casino job experience resets to zero. Jail always ends: you earn a $50-per-day credit against the tax owed, and you're released once the balance is paid. There is no permanent game over — you can always rebuild, including through the casino job.

What happens if a stock goes bankrupt?

If you hold shares in a company that goes bankrupt, the long shares are wiped out (the loss is recorded for tax purposes), and if you were short the stock, the short profits. The bankrupt company is replaced by a new company, so the market always has fresh opportunities.

How do dividends work?

Dividend-paying stocks pay daily: your shares × the stock price × its dividend yield, divided by 252. ETFs pay daily distributions the same way, and eREIT funds pay daily dividends on your shares. Cash sitting idle also earns a daily sweep: the Fed rate minus 0.50% (with a 0.50% floor), accrued daily and taxed as ordinary income.

What are the fees and costs?

There are no commissions on stock trades, but every sale pays a spread that depends on the company's size tier: 0.02% for the largest companies up to 0.60% for the smallest. Options are priced with Black-Scholes and a volatility smile — no separate commission. ETFs charge a daily expense-ratio drag. Real estate sales pay a 4% closing fee. Small-business financial reports cost 1% of the price. Sentinel stock analysis reports cost $10 each. Managers and robot traders charge rent and/or profit commissions as shown on their cards. Margin borrowing, credit-line borrowing, and mortgages all charge interest tied to the game's Fed rate.

Can I short stocks?

Yes. Selling short requires closing any long position in the same stock first. You post collateral equal to the full dollar cost from your buying power, and you pay a daily borrow cost based on the stock's short interest and dividend yield. You close a short by buying the shares back.

How do options work in the game?

You can buy (long) or write (short) calls and puts on stocks, with expirations of 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, 360, or 720 days, in Mini lots (10 shares) or Standard lots (100 shares). Prices use Black-Scholes with a volatility smile and term structure, and the game shows you the Greeks (delta, gamma, vega, theta). Writing an option requires collateral of 20% of the strike × lot size × contracts, and the premium lands in your cash immediately. Options are cash-settled at expiry: long options pay out their intrinsic value, and written options pay out from your cash — no shares change hands. You can close a position early. Options trade 8:30 AM–3:00 PM game time. For strategy guidance, see the Premium Harvest guide.

How does real estate work?

There are 21,500 properties across 10 tiers, from micro condos to trophy assets. Buy with 20% down plus a mortgage (10–30 year terms, rate locked at purchase, based on the Fed rate plus a spread) or pay 100% cash. Rental income is collected daily, minus property management fees, property tax and insurance, and mortgage payments. Property managers come in tiers — from Listing Only (3%) up to Master Lease (18%, with full auto-pilot perks) and a hotel-only short-term tier. You can renovate (star levels 1–5), refinance, pay down extra principal, and sell (4% closing fee, mortgage payoff, gains taxed in the long-term bucket). New construction adds a few properties each quarter.

What are eREITs?

eREITs are 24 private real-estate funds, Fundrise-style. Shares cost roughly $1,000 of net asset value each, with limited supply per fund. You invest by dollars (minimum $500 converts to shares) and earn daily dividends. The catch is liquidity: redemptions are quarterly — you request a sale and it settles at the next quarter close (every 63 trading days). Each fund has property managers and star rankings.

How do small businesses work?

There are 52,000 small businesses priced from $5,000 to $10,000,000. Purchases are 100% cash only — no financing and no margin. Each business generates daily cash flow, which can be positive or negative, and is taxed as ordinary income (or booked as investment losses). A weekly management step can turn businesses around or bankrupt them. You can buy a 30-day financial report for 1% of the price before deciding. Bankrupt businesses are removed and replaced. You can list a business for sale at a premium or discount — the deeper the discount, the higher the daily chance an AI buyer takes it.

How does venture capital work?

Venture capital requires a $1,000,000 net worth ("accredited investor" gate). There are 28 startups across five stages, from Seed Round (minimum $10,000 check) to Pre-IPO Late (minimum $100,000). Each startup shows a failure risk, a target IPO multiple, and a milestone countdown. Your investment is illiquid until the startup IPOs or fails — there is no way to sell early. At IPO, your stake converts to public stock shares at the IPO multiple. If the startup fails, you lose the full amount invested.

How do forex and commodities work?

Forex offers 8 currency pairs (including gold and silver pairs and Bitcoin) with leverage from 10x to 100x and a $5 minimum margin. You can go long or short, and positions earn or pay daily swap based on the interest-rate difference between the two currencies, minus a financing drag. If your equity falls to 10% of your posted margin, the position is stopped out. Commodities offer 16 markets (gold, oil, wheat, coffee, and more) with a $5 minimum, either spot (1x) or 5x futures, long-only, with partial closes. Supply cycles affect commodity prices. Both markets trade around the clock with no hours gate.

What is the casino? Can I lose everything there?

The casino has 11 games — Blackjack, Baccarat, Rocket, Chess vs. the Old Grandmaster, Viking vs. Samurai, European Roulette, Texas Hold'em, 4-Row Royal Gold Slots, Scratch-Offs, Horse Racing, and NASCAR — plus a Mega Lottery and 5 sports-betting machines. Casino bets use your game cash, so yes, you can lose real (virtual) money there, and the odds favor the house like any casino. The Mega Lottery costs $5 per ticket with a $100,000,000 jackpot drawn daily; secondary prizes range from $5,000 to $1,000,000. You can also work in the casino: $10/hour for an 8-hour shift ($80/day), with a $1/hour raise every game year up to $1,000/day — but while you're working, all new investments are locked, and the moment you stop working, all your job experience resets to zero.

Who are the AI rivals?

100 AI players compete with you on the leaderboard, each following one of 9 archetypes — Mega/Value, Momentum, Options Pro, Mini-Cap Hunter, Real Estate Mogul, Macro / Gold, Long/Short, Dividend & Rent, and YOLO Catalyst. They trade, invest, pay taxes, and can even go to jail. You can inspect any rival's dossier to see their strategy, holdings, and activity log. New rivals spawn over time. Your rank on the leaderboard is the game's ongoing competition.

What are managers and robot traders?

Managers invest your money for you. There are 5,200 robot traders (minimum $500 allocation, monthly rent from $4.99) and human portfolio managers, wealth groups, and trading firms (minimum $10,000; Professional managers require $500,000+ net worth, Elite require $10,000,000+). Managers charge profit commissions, and you control a reserve percentage, withdrawals, firing, and automation. Aggressive high-target managers carry blow-up risk — an account can be wiped out. Managed accounts can also be seized for unpaid taxes.

Is there a way to win? What happens when I run out of money?

There is no win condition and no game over. InveSim is an endless sandbox: the competition is your net-worth rank against the 100 AI rivals and your own growth over time. If you lose everything, you can always rebuild — the casino job pays from day one, and jail always releases you. The game never ends and never deletes your progress unless you start a new game.

How do I start a new game?

Settings → Start New Game. You'll choose a 6-digit seed (random or your own — the seed generates your entire market universe, so a different seed means different companies, prices, and rivals) and an initial economic regime: Balanced (default), Tech Boom, Stagflation, or High Volatility, each with different Fed rates, inflation, GDP, and volatility. Starting capital is always $10,000. Note: you can't start a new game while a casino bet is active — resign the bet first.
