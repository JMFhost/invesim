About InveSim

InveSim is a free investment simulation game. Players start with $10,000 of virtual money and learn how investing works by trading virtual stocks, ETFs, options, real estate, small businesses, commodities and forex — plus a virtual casino — against AI rivals, with no real money at stake.

The game is built and run by Jesse Pham (JMFhost). It is a learning and entertainment project: all prices, companies, properties and market events inside the game are simulated for play, and nothing in the game is investment advice.
